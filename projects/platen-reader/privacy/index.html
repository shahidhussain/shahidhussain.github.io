Privacy

Platen doesn’t collect any data about you or what you read. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

The app only goes online for two things:

  • The Mac version checks this site for updates.
  • If you ask for read aloud’s natural voice, the app downloads it once from GitHub. It never does this unless you ask.

Neither request carries anything that identifies you. Once the voice is downloaded, read aloud runs entirely on your device.

Your books never leave your device unless you keep them somewhere that syncs, such as iCloud Drive.

Questions? See support.
