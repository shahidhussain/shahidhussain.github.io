Privacy and support

Privacy

Platen doesn’t collect any data about you or what you read. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

The app only goes online for two things: the Mac version checks this site for updates, and both versions download the list of free books from it. Neither request carries anything that identifies you. Read aloud runs entirely on your device.

Your books never leave your device unless you keep them somewhere that syncs, such as iCloud Drive.

Support

Found a bug, or a book that doesn’t look right? Email platen-reader@shahidhussain.com, and attach the file if you can. Odd books make the most useful reports.

If you use GitHub, you can also open an issue at github.com/shahidhussain/platen-reader/issues.

Platen opens DRM-free EPUB files only. Books bought from Kindle or Apple Books are locked to those apps, and Platen can’t open them.
