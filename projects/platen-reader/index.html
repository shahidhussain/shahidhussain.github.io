Open a book. Start reading.

Platen Reader is a free, open source EPUB viewer for Mac and iPhone. Double-click a file and you’re back on the page where you left off. No library, no importing, no account.

Your files stay where they are

Open a book from Finder, iCloud Drive or a network share. Platen reads it in place. Nothing gets copied into a hidden library, so there’s nothing to tidy up later.

Books look the way they were made

Platen respects the publisher’s typography by default. Change the font, margins or spacing if you like, and switch any change off again.

Nothing to sign up for

Free and open source under MPL 2.0. No account, no subscription and no tracking.

Platen opens DRM-free EPUB files. Books bought from Kindle or Apple Books can’t be opened. Looking for something to read? The app includes a list of free, well-made editions from Standard Ebooks and Project Gutenberg.
